# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Women looking for beginner-friendly Islamic classes, including complete beginners, reverts and those wanting a refresher. Many feel shy or worried it is too late to start.
- Parents looking for classes for their children.
- The client, Mualimah Rukeya, who updates programmes and workshops herself.

## Product Purpose
The website for Beginners Madrasah Classes (est. 2024): Islamic classes for women and children, online and in person. It explains the programmes, builds trust in the teacher, and gets people to register. Success means registrations and enquiries.

## Positioning
A warm, safe, beginner-friendly learning space, taught by Mualimah Rukeya, that is affordable and flexible (self-paced, live online and in-person options).

## Operating Context
- Pages: Home, About, Programmes (with class pages), Testimonials, FAQ, Contact.
- Six programmes, from the site's content: Women's Online Madrasah (R150/month, self-paced), Women's Hifdh Programme (R200/month, live online), Children's Online Madrasah (R150/month, ages 6 to 13), Online Workshops & Short Courses (from R100), Get Into Qur'an: In Person (R200/month, Cape Town), In-Person Workshops (from R100).
- Programmes and workshops are edited by the client through TinaCMS (`content/`). Prices and programme details live there and are never hard-coded from memory.
- Hosted on Netlify. Live at https://beginnersmadrasah.co.za.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pro bono client site for Digital Pantry Web Studio (real external client).
- Islamic content (terms, transliteration, class subjects, du'as) must be accurate and is flagged for the founder's review before it goes live.
- Registration and contact forms collect personal details, including about children, so POPIA applies and data is kept to the minimum.

## Brand Commitments
- Beginners Madrasah Classes' own brand, not the Digital Pantry look. Has its own illustrated mascot.
- Tone: warm, gentle, respectful and plain. No cheeky copy on faith content.

## Evidence on Hand
- Student testimonials on the site are real and supplied by the client (confirmed by the founder, 2026-10-10). Use them as written; do not edit their meaning or add new ones.
- Photos of the teacher and classes are in the site assets.
- Claims about student numbers come from the client's own copy. Do not add new figures.

## Product Principles
1. Make a nervous beginner feel welcome and safe.
2. Clear about what each programme is, who it is for, when and how much.
3. Accuracy and respect in all faith content come before style.
4. The client can keep it up to date herself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
HQ standards apply (`knowledge/tech/STANDARDS.md`): accessibility and Core Web Vitals are non-negotiable. Mobile-first, readable for all ages.
